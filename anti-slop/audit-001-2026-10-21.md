## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-02 em dash in copy.** `components/ProductDetails.tsx:294` ("hardware—no full kit") and `:445` ("full kit—our individual"). Replace with a comma or colon.
2. **R-25 / R-34 contrast, inline colours ignore the light theme.** The cart summary is `#f9fafb` in light mode, but its inline text uses dark-theme colours: `#9ca3af` (about 2.4:1) at `app/cart/page.tsx:536` and `#38bdf8` (about 2:1) at `app/cart/page.tsx:545`. The same `#38bdf8` "Processing PayPal payment..." text is at `components/PayPalCheckoutButton.tsx:41`, and `#9ca3af` placeholder text is at `app/cart/page.tsx:207`. Move these into CSS with a `prefers-color-scheme` pair like the rest of `styles/cart.css`.
3. **R-32 weak focus indicator on inputs.** `styles/cart.css:317` and `styles/about.css:489` remove the outline and only change a 1px border colour. Add a visible `:focus-visible` ring.
4. **R-37 no design direction.** There is no `DESIGN.md`. Future UI work has no stated direction to filter against.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

5. **R-04 generic AI icon.** `Sparkles` at `app/cart/page.tsx:283` and `components/ProductDetails.tsx:270` (also imported in `components/Header.tsx`). It decorates promo/launch messages; a `Tag` or no icon says the same thing. More broadly, every icon is Lucide; that's fine where the meaning is literal (cart, truck, chevrons), but it's worth a written reason.
6. **R-08 arrows on CTAs.** "Shop All Products" and "View All Products" both carry `ArrowRight` (`app/page.tsx`). Keep one if it marks the primary action; drop it from the secondary.
7. **R-10 blur with no effect.** `styles/header.css:10` sets `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` on a header whose background is opaque `#111827`, so nothing is ever blurred. Remove it.
8. **R-06 typeface without a reason.** `app/globals.css:12` is the default system stack. Fine if chosen for speed or neutrality; write that down, or pick a face that suits an automotive parts brand.
9. **R-01 announcement bar gradient.** `styles/header.css:16` is a blue-to-blue gradient. It's subtle; keep it if it's the brand colour, otherwise a flat `#0284c7` reads the same.
